Read this as spheres of coverage, not a reporting chart. Placement, ordering, and grouping imply no rank. Authority, responsibility, and accountability are deliberately not fixed here — that work belongs to the decision-making matrix and the policies & procedures groups, and the pathways shown are drawn from their working drafts.

Committees (pedagogical and board) are not shown as standing bodies: each committee's responsibility, authority, and accountability are scoped at its creation.

The Parent Teacher Council is one body with three touchpoints — class reps · co-chairs & treasurer · rep to board. Selecting any PTC role traces the thread between all three.
